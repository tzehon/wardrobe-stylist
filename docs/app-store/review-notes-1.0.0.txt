2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Wardrobe Stylist helps individuals organize their own clothes and choose outfits from items they already own. Users keep a private wardrobe catalog, optionally request an AI outfit suggestion, and record what they wear. It is a consumer lifestyle utility, not an enterprise-only app, marketplace or social network. Version 1.0.0 is Free, with no in-app purchases, subscriptions or paid features.

3. SETUP AND MAIN FEATURES
No human account, registration, login, review credentials, API key or sample-file import is required.

Offline tour: On first launch, choose “Try the offline demo”. The “Demo Mode · Fictional Data” banner identifies disposable sample data. Browse/edit Wardrobe items, view the bundled Today outfit and sample History. Demo Settings allows reset or exit. This tour makes no network AI request.

Connected flow: Exit Demo Mode and choose “Start with my wardrobe”. Add a white cotton top, navy trousers and white sneakers manually in Wardrobe. Users may also add their own clothing photos through Camera or Photo Library. Open Settings → Connected Features, read the disclosure and tap “Allow AI styling”. In Today, tap “Style a look”. An optional occasion can be entered for a restyle. “Wear this” saves the look to local History.

Optional AI requires internet and Apple App Attest on a physical iPhone. App Attest verifies this app installation, not a human account. Styling is subject to service usage limits. If verification or the service is unavailable, the app displays a recovery message; local catalog and Demo remain usable.

Wardrobe entries/photos and History are stored locally; there is no user-to-user posting, messaging, public feed or user discovery. There are consequently no public-content reporting or user-blocking flows. Settings → Privacy & Data contains separate local-data and server-security-data deletion controls. No account-deletion flow applies because there is no human account. Optional local reminders open Today without generating an outfit in the background.

4. SERVICES AND PLATFORMS
- Fly.io hosts the developer-operated HTTPS backend.
- Anthropic Claude supplies optional, explicitly requested AI outfit suggestions.
- Apple App Attest provides anonymous installation verification for backend access.
- Native iOS Camera/Photos and local notification APIs support optional photo capture/selection and reminders.

Only disclosed compact wardrobe text, recent-wear/rating summaries and optional occasion/context text are sent for styling after consent and an explicit styling action. Wardrobe photos are excluded. The backend does not persist wardrobe, prompt or model-response payloads. The privacy policy explains provider retention and separate minimal security/aggregate budget records. No payment processor, social login or external retail catalog is used.

5. REGIONAL BEHAVIOR
This version is distributed only in Singapore. It has no country-specific feature or content variants; normal device locale/timezone formatting applies.

6. REGULATED SERVICES AND CONTENT RIGHTS
This is a lifestyle wardrobe organizer, not a regulated professional service. It does not distribute a third-party retail or media catalog. Bundled examples are fictional. The developer confirms the necessary rights for bundled artwork/text and permitted use of user-supplied photos. Users select their own clothing photos; those photos remain on device and are not sent for AI styling.
